Privacy

Tracuela · last updated 5 November 2026

Tracuela is an app for bands: it holds the structure of your songs and the recordings you make against them. This page sets out exactly what it stores, where, and who else can see it. It is deliberately short, because the app does not do very much with your data.

What there is never any of

  • No advertising. There is no ad network in the app and none planned.
  • No tracking or product analytics. Nothing records which screens you open or how long you spend in them.
  • No selling or sharing your data with anyone, for any purpose.
  • No push notifications, so no device tokens are collected.

What is stored

WhatWhy
Your email address, and a display name if you have one To sign you in and to show your bandmates who changed a song
Your bands, songs, arrangements, lyrics, notes and setlists They are the thing the app is for
Audio recordings you make or import So the band can hear a take against the song it belongs to
Crash reports So faults get fixed. These contain technical details about the failure, not your songs.

Recordings are personal data and treated as such. They are audible to the members of the band the song belongs to, and to nobody else.

The microphone

The app asks for the microphone only so you can record takes, and only records while you have pressed record. It does not listen at any other time. You can refuse the permission and still use everything else.

Sign-in

There are no passwords. You sign in with Apple, with Google, or with a one-time code sent to your email. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, Tracuela only ever sees the relay address Apple gives it.

Deleting things

Deleting a band deletes its songs and its recordings.

You can delete your account from inside the app — Band → Account → Delete account. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

What happens to your bands

Bands you share with other people carry on without you. They keep their songs, arrangements and recordings; your name simply stops appearing beside them. A band nobody else is in is deleted along with your account.

If you are the only admin of a band that still has other members, the app asks you to choose who takes over before you go — or to delete that band instead. A band with no admin could not be managed by anyone, so it is not a state we will leave behind.

What happens to your recordings

By default, takes you recorded stay with the band, without your name attached. A recording is usually part of a shared arrangement, and removing it would take a part out of songs your bandmates are still working on. They can delete any take themselves.

If you would rather your recordings went with you, there is a checkbox on the deletion screen that removes every take you made, from every song. It is your performance, so it is your call — we would rather offer the choice than decide it for you.

A recording you replace or remove during ordinary use is marked deleted immediately and stops being available in the app. The underlying file is removed from storage afterwards rather than at that instant. Recordings deleted as part of deleting your account are removed from storage at the time.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email the address above and you will get an answer from a person.

Children

Tracuela is not aimed at children under 13 and accounts are not knowingly created for them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the app will say so rather than hoping you re-read the page.
